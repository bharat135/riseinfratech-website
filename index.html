Who we are

Concrete strength.
Rooted in Panvel.

RISE INFRATECH is an infrastructure and construction-materials company based in Panvel, Raigad. The RMC lifecycle

From quarry to pour.

Five controlled stages stand between raw material and the concrete in your structure. Nothing leaves the plant until every one is signed off.

  1. STEP 01

    Procurement

    Aggregates, cement, fly-ash, GGBS and admixtures sourced from approved vendors and tested on arrival.

  2. STEP 02

    Batching

    Computerised weigh-batching doses every ingredient exactly to the approved mix design.

  3. STEP 03

    QA/QC Lab

    Slump, temperature and cube samples are taken from each batch before it is cleared for dispatch.

  4. STEP 04

    Transit Mixer

    GPS-tracked mixers keep the concrete agitated and on the clock, from plant gate to site gate.

  5. STEP 05

    Delivery

    Placed by pump or chute, with batch tickets and test reports handed over at site.

Sustainability & Quality

Greener mixes.
Longer lives.

The most sustainable structure is the one that never needs rebuilding. We replace a share of cement with fly-ash and GGBS, industrial by-products that cut embodied carbon while making concrete denser, cooler-curing and more resistant to the chlorides and sulphates of the Konkan coast.
